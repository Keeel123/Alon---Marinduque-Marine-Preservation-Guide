✳ A COASTAL COMMUNITY GUIDE FOR MARINDUQUE

For barangays, fishing communities, classrooms, and volunteers.

01 / Caring for Marinduque's marine life

A little more care
goes a long way.

Our reefs, mangroves, seagrass, and marine wildlife support coastal communities across Marinduque. These practical habits help residents, fishers, and visitors care for them.

01⌁

ON OUR SHORELINE

Leave nature where it is

Take your rubbish home. Leave shells, coral, and small sea creatures in their habitat, and use established paths near mangroves and coastal vegetation.

02◒

AROUND MARINE WILDLIFE

Watch gently, give space

If you spot marine wildlife, observe quietly from a distance. Never chase, touch, feed, or take flash photos. Share sightings with your community without disturbing the animal.

03♧

AT HOME AND IN THE BARANGAY

Keep waterways clean

Use reusables, follow your barangay's waste collection and sorting guidance, and never pour oil, paint, or chemicals into drains, creeks, or the sea.

04≈

FISHING AND BUYING SEAFOOD

Support responsible local fishing

Fishers can share knowledge of local waters and responsible practices. When buying seafood, ask where and how it was caught, choose legal local catch, and follow applicable seasonal and size limits.

i

See stranded or injured wildlife? Keep a safe distance and inform your barangay or municipal government so they can connect with the appropriate environment or fisheries office. Do not attempt to handle the animal yourself.

03 / Healthy seas, strong local livelihoods

A living sea.
A livelihood, too.

Marinduque's coastal livelihoods and healthy marine ecosystems go hand in hand. Practical, sustainable choices can help keep local food, work, and community opportunities going.

01 — FISHING & SEAFOOD≈

Value local fishers
and responsible catch.

Support local fishing households when you can. Ask about catch methods and choose seafood caught in line with local rules.

02 — VISITING OUR COAST↗

Welcome visitors
with care.

Choose local guides and services, respect protected areas, and follow barangay rules at beaches, reefs, and mangroves.

03 — COMMUNITY ACTION✳

Make room for
every generation.

Students and volunteers can join barangay-approved shoreline clean-ups, learn from local fishers, support community conservation, and share useful information with neighbours.

One Marinduque. Many hands.

Let's make care
part of the everyday.

Choose one safe, practical action this week. Ask your barangay about local activities, or invite a classmate, neighbour, fisher, or fellow volunteer to join in.
