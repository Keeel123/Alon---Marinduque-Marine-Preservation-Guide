Small steps, real impact

Care for the ocean,
wherever you are.

Protecting marine life doesn't have to be complicated. Start with a habit that fits your day.

AT HOME

Skip single-use plastics

Carry a reusable bottle and bag. Recycle correctly, and keep wrappers and cigarette butts off the street.

Make it a habit ↗

BY THE WATER

Leave the shore as you found it

Take your rubbish with you, stick to marked paths, and leave shells and sea life where they belong.

Explore shore care ↗

IN YOUR COMMUNITY

Choose seafood thoughtfully

Ask where your seafood comes from and look for options caught or farmed with care.

Ask a good question ↗

When you visit the coast

Look closely.
Leave gently.

Watch wildlife quietly, give nesting and resting animals room, and never feed or touch wild marine life. Your calm presence helps keep them safe.

Be a thoughtful visitor ↗

travel further.
